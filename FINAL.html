The person behind the vision

Owner & Founder

Meet the leadership behind BEY THAI LEAGUE and its mission to connect basketball talent with international opportunity.

Tray Bey

Founder & Commissioner

Leadership · Vision · Opportunity

Trayvon Jefferson Bey

Founder & Commissioner | Bey Thai League

Trayvon Jefferson Bey, widely known as Tray Bey, is the Founder and Commissioner of Bey Thai League and the Owner and Founder of Bey Sports Agency & Consulting (BSA).

With an international basketball background spanning multiple continents, Tray Bey established Bey Thai League to create new opportunities for athletes seeking professional exposure, international experience, and pathways to advance their basketball careers.

300+Athletes placed internationally through Bey Sports Agency, connecting players with opportunities across Asia, Europe, Latin America, and other professional basketball markets.

Through his work with Bey Sports Agency, he has helped place 300+ athletes internationally, connecting players with opportunities across Asia, Europe, Latin America, and other professional basketball markets.

His vision for Bey Thai League goes beyond competition. The league is designed to provide athletes with a platform to showcase their talents through organized competition, professional-level coaching, FIBA-standard statistics, high-quality game film, and international basketball exposure.

As a basketball entrepreneur and league commissioner, Tray Bey continues to build connections between players, coaches, teams, sponsors, and basketball organizations worldwide.

His Vision

Connecting Talent With Opportunity

To build an international basketball platform that connects talent with opportunity, expands the global reach of the game, and creates meaningful pathways for athletes to pursue their professional basketball dreams.

The Mission

Develop. Create. Connect.

Developing talent. Creating opportunities. Connecting the world through basketball.
